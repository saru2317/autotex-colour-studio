AUTOTEX — PHP / CPANEL EDITION

Open INSTALL.html for illustrated installation instructions.

1. Extract this package in your cPanel Home directory, outside public_html.
2. Put autotex-private alongside public_html, not inside it.
3. Copy public_html/colour-studio into your site's existing public_html folder.
   Include hidden .htaccess and .user.ini files.
4. Enable PHP 8.3 or later (minimum 8.2) and HTTPS.
5. Open https://YOUR-DOMAIN/colour-studio/setup.php and create your admin account
   using the private setup key in autotex-private/setup-key.txt.
6. Sign in at /colour-studio/admin.php, upload a STEP model, choose colours and save.

No Node.js, database, Composer, Cloudflare or ChatGPT account is needed on the server.
A developer-only frontend-source folder is included for future changes.
Do not place this ZIP, the private folder, or your backups in the public website folder.
The package is a fresh installation. Hosted preview uploads are not included.
